THE OPERATOR

I'm Sameer — a videographer obsessed with the frame between science fiction and documentary truth.

For eight years I've directed and shot films for automotive, fashion and tech brands, chasing light from Himalayan passes to server-room neon. My toolkit spans cinema cameras, FPV drones, robotic motion control and a color suite tuned for the dark.

Every project is treated like a world: designed, lit and scored to pull the viewer somewhere they haven't been.
